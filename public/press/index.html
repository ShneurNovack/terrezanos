Press

Terrezano's and Domenico's have each been on national television once. Both times, we were told it was a commercial.

Saturday Night Live, 2017

Terrezano's made its television debut in "Italian Restaurant," which aired on the Season 43 premiere of Saturday Night Live on September 30, 2017, the same night we opened. Three couples taste the pasta and love it. Then a man in a suit explains where the pasta actually came from, and one couple takes the news very personally.

Saturday Night Live, 2018

One year later, almost to the day, Domenico's appeared in "Coffee Shop" on the Season 44 premiere, September 29, 2018. Three couples love their Americanos until a familiar man in a suit arrives with another announcement. A pair of newlyweds, one day married, do not take it well.
