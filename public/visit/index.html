Getting here

Subway
B, D, F or M to 47-50 Sts Rockefeller Center, then a three-minute walk east.
Parking
Garages on West 49th and West 50th Streets.
Access
Step-free entrances at both 43 and 44 Rockefeller Lane, and an accessible restroom on the dining room level.
Delivery
We do not deliver, and never have.

Terrezano's and Domenico's are fictional restaurants from two Saturday Night Live sketches, "Italian Restaurant" (September 30, 2017) and "Coffee Shop" (September 29, 2018). This is a fan tribute. The chef, the family, the addresses, the phone numbers and the guests quoted here are invented, and no reservation is ever booked.
