Domenico's Caffè

A Neapolitan espresso bar next door to Terrezano's, run by Luigi's youngest brother, Domenico Marinara. Open every morning from 7.

44 Rockefeller Lane, New York. Telephone (212) 555-0929.

Domenico knows coffee

Domenico Marinara was born in Torre del Greco in 1975, the last of five. While his brother Claudio was learning to cook, Domenico was learning to pull espresso, first at the bar on the corner of their street and then for twenty years in the old caffès along Via Toledo in Naples, where the coffee is short, dark and sweet and the customers have very strong opinions.

He followed Luigi to New York in the summer of 2018 with a lever espresso machine in a crate and opened Domenico's next door to the restaurant on Saturday, September 29, 2018. The coffee is roasted dark in the Neapolitan style by a small roaster in Brooklyn and is made from coffee beans and nothing else.

House customs

Caffè sospeso
The Neapolitan tradition of paying for a second coffee for whoever comes in next and cannot afford one. Ask at the register if there is one waiting.
Newlyweds
Domenico's very first customers were a couple the morning after their wedding. They ordered two Americanos and called it the best coffee of their lives. Ever since, any couple in on the day after their wedding drinks for free.
Our regulars
The ones who come every morning have a name for themselves. Ask one. They will tell you, loudly.
The baristas
All three trained with Domenico, and every one of them is a real barista. Please do not call them batistas.
